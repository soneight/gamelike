## ROGUE

### Multiplayer
> mostly just randomly selling andor buying items between players, off-line mode just transfer items to sell andor buy randomly

### Modes

- Progression:
  - `Like`: no, just user experience and luck
  - `Lite`: some progression persist between generated runs, mostly environmental
- Difficulty:
  - `Hard`: just one life
  - `Soft`: allow revive previous dead body, but if dead again run is over
- Trade:
  - `Solo`: solo-self found, no trading between players
  - `Rand`: just `RANDomized` trading, players can choose items to by with a fee and it sell randomly between top 20 percent of prices, or randomly do not sell at all
