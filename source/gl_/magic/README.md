## MAGIC

### Archetypes
> each of listed archetypes would be have hero with same name, mono would have two heroes

Likes are formed based on earlier versions of inspirational games

- `Control`: like `dimir` from `MTG` or `Warrior` from `HS`
- `Burn/Face`: like `mono-red` from `MTG` or `Hunter` form `HS`
- `Ramp/Grow`: like `mono-green` from `MTG` or `Druid` from `HS`
- `Draw/Mill`: like `mono-blue` from `MTG` or `Warlock` from `HS`
- `Aggro`: like `gruul` form `MTG` or `` form `HS`
- `Mezzo`: like `simic` from `MTG` or `Paladin` from `HS`
- `Tempo`: like `izzet` from `MTG` or `Mage` from `HS`
- `Combo`: like `esper` or `Rogue` from `HS`
